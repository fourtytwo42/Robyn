## Finding and remediation

### UAT-2026-07-23-01 — Portfolio reward claim was not actionable

- Severity: medium usability blocker.
- Expected: the claimable reward amounts displayed in Portfolio have a local claim action.
- Actual: the claim was possible only from the homepage development console.
- Remediation: `PortfolioView` now simulates and submits `claimAll()` per launch,
  waits for the receipt, reports the transaction state, and refreshes authoritative
  balances. The button is disabled when the selected token has no claimable reward.
- Regression: strict web typecheck and optimized build are required before promotion;
  browser acceptance must prove the Portfolio button against a newly claimable token.

## Independent persona limitation

Three explicitly requested testing subagents were started for new-user/creator,
trader/DAO, and mobile/wallet personas. Their isolated runtimes reported that the
Codex in-app browser was unavailable and provided no wallet, transaction, screenshot,
or usability evidence. They were not replaced with Chrome because the release requires
the in-app browser. The serialized root-agent workflows above are valid engineering
acceptance but do not satisfy the independent multi-persona gate. That gate remains
open until independent browser-capable runtimes are available or the product owner
explicitly waives it.
